So, the U.S. maintains its military, so that no other conventional military force will dream of attacking it. But since no-one is going to attack the U.S., because of its military, what should the U.S. military spend its time doing?

Do you know what the one thing that no military training can actually teach?

What it feels like to know that another human being is actually, actively trying to kill you.

You can create all sorts of exercise situations and simulations, in real and virtual space, you can even use live ammunition in carefully controlled environments. But until you put your soldiers in a situation where someone is, very actually, actively trying to kill them, you have no way of knowing how they will react, and how they will perform.

To keep a military force in a full state of preparedness, and especially to keep that military force at the very top of the chart of military forces, requires making sure that they have the best and most advanced equipment possible.

The way to do that is to make sure that the military has the greatest and most advanced industrial and technological infrastructure providing it with the most advanced equipment available.

This creates a complex relationship between the military itself, and the industrial and technical facilities behind it. The military industry needs to keep ahead of the game. It requires an awful lot of money to keep it producing the best and most advanced equipment. The money for research and development in military technology comes from the revenue generated from the sale of existing military technology, arms and armaments.

If the military-industrial complex is going to keep itself at the top of the game, it needs the revenue generated by Wars and fighting. To establish the effectiveness of new technology, it requires that the new technology be battle-tested. For just as the fighting ability of an individual human can not be finally established until they have been exposed to hostile enemy fire; neither can a new piece of military hardware, or a new type of weapon be considered reliable and effective, until it too has been battle-tested.

So, the result is what we see in the United States of America.

And being constantly at War, is the only way that the �Defence� force can make sure it never needs to be at War.
