I'm not really able to respond objectively to what you have written SG, because I have a pathological case of [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confirmation_bias]Confirmation Bias[/url] when it comes to this topic.

But I *CAN* respond Subjectively! 8-)

I had a personal Experience at age 17 that felt more Real than anything I had experienced up to that point.

I felt I had died.

I believed I had died.

And that's all it takes for an Experience to be a "Near-Death Experience", or "NDE": that the individual who Experienced it believed they died.

Lots of individuals have NDEs. Some people have lots of NDEs in a short space of Time.

Front-Line Military personnel for example, are exposed to a tremendous psychological pressure while being so close to their own potential deaths. Terminally-ill patients have fairly frequent NDEs as they near the end of their Time.

I recall when Holywoman's father was just a few weeks away from the end of his Time with us. He was receiving hospice care in a facility not far from where we live, when HW got the call that he had died, but that the staff had been able to revive him.

When I next saw him, about 18 hours after that event, he was absolutely *BUZZING*!

His mind was alive with all of the Truth that he had Understood during his Time without his mind being in the way of that Understanding.

He wanted to talk it all through with the World! He wanted me to organise a press conference, and to arrange security for him with a mutual associate at the local club where we played snooker together...

I got where he was coming from. I could relate to how he was feeling. He had seen how easily Everything does and can function effectively. How all of the Knowledge is readily available, and how we're mostly trying to do Everything backwards and/or upside down.

And he wanted to share his Elation with the World.

It is this emotional response that is the common and critical factor with NDEs. Common across all NDEs, regardless of the cultural prism through which they are experienced.

This [url=https://nderf.org/index.htm]NDERF website[/url] (Near-Death Experience Research Foundation) has some really interesting articles on this topic.

Subjectively:

I believe I exist in order to Understand through Experience, what I already Know to be True about my Self.

I can and will keep Existing as an Individual through as many successive "Life Experiences" as I feel I need to completely Understand my Self. Cumulatively building my Understanding by allowing my Life Experiences to be guided by my Emotions, and never impeded by my thoughts.
