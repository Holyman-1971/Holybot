So, I'm designing a new MMORPG.

[i](Well I'm not actually, but just pretend I am.)[/i]

It's going to be set on this World dominated mostly by apes (at least inasmuch as the apes believe they dominate it), but shared with all manner of other fauna, flora and fungi.

About three-quarters of the World will be ocean, and the rest land, with various climates from arctic to desert. The apes' level of technological advancement should be familiar to most players, and the skills to use the technology will come easy to the intuitive.

Obviously I've got a lot of Design work to do as Intelligently as possible, before I can get on with Creating the Game World, and spurred on by another thread (and also by Francois), I figured I'd start with designing the Skills/Professions/Occupations Matrix for the apes.

In order to support a functioning, stable and Peaceful foundation for Social Development and Advancement, I need to make sure the occupations chosen by the players/apes correspond with a fair reward system.

At least to begin with, the numbers of vacancies for each profession/occupation will be limited; and the professions with the highest rewards will also require the greatest qualification process. Furthermore, following initial qualification for an occupation, keeping the occupation will also be subject to on-going performance evaluations. Otherwise, the player/apes will cram hard to qualify for an occupation, but once they have tenure, they might be tempted to allow their performance to drop-off.

The idea, of course, is to make sure that the *CURRENTLY* most qualified player/apes are always in the appropriate occupation. It would destabilise and imbalance the Game World if a player/ape who was no longer effective in an occupation/role, was preventing another player/ape who would be more effective and capable, from performing the role.

Now, to ensure that the most capable and effective players/apes are carrying out the most important roles (important to Ape Society as a Whole), I need to establish an appropriate occupation-reward matrix.

Which is where you guys come in.

Below are listed 100 professions. If you can spare the Time, rank each profession according to how much value each one brings to *SOCIETY AS A WHOLE*. Do this by ranking each profession 1 to 50, where 1 is the most important/most valuable social role, and 50 is the least important/possibly detrimental social role.

There may be some occupations/professions missing from the list that you feel should have been included, but I need to keep things limited and concise for the Launch. Additional roles may be created as the Game develops, according to the emerging requirements of the Game Society.

Feel free to discuss and argue about the importance or unimportance of each role. Equally, feel free to throw pie, call me a faggot, or divert the thread off into a polemic about your own political or religious ideology: this is =PBF=, after all.

Obviously I could have just created a thread where I asked what roles you considered most important to current Human Society, and cut straight to the chase. But this *IS* =PBF=, I *AM* Holyman, and it *IS* Friday Afternoon.

So please don't waste your Time pointing out the bleeding obvious.

OK. I've listed the 100 professions/occupations in the post below, and grouped them in vaguely associated groups to help you prioritise them. Reply by quoting the list, and ranking it by putting the numbers 1 to 100 inclusive, against each profession.

And when you've done, sign-off by also selecting the profession/occupation *YOU* would select in the Game World.

I'll keep a running tally of the consensus, and post that every 5 or 10 responses or so; and keep going until the thread dies from lack of interest.
