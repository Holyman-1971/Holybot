Right then: it is my Experience that God is [b]*NOT*[/b] a player in Existence.

God just Is.

God doesn't mind if we live lives of beneficent altruism, or sociopathic rage.

God doesn't have a stake in the Game of Life.

I use "God" just as a shorthand term for Everything that possibly, probably or potentially Exists. If you can think of it, imagine it, feel it, conceive of it, then "It" is part of "God".

"God" equals Everything There Is.

"God" does [b]*NOT*[/b] equal any limiting religious definition that we may be aware of, or care about (though all those definitions are aspects of "God" too, since we can think and imagine them to be so).

Right then.

"God", in Its Infinite Facility and Capacity for Conceptual ability, wanted to understand what "God" is.

"God" [b]*KNOWS*[/b] what "God" is ([b]*EVERYTHING*[/b]),  but "God" cannot [b]*UNDERSTAND*[/b] what it meant to be "God"/Everything; because it is impossible for "God" to Experience what it means to be "Not God".

Everything = "God"; nothing = "Not God".

To have knowledge of something, is to be informed of or about something.

To [b]*UNDERSTAND*[/b] something, is to have [b]*EXPERIENCE*[/b] of that something.

To Understand the Existence of something, is to have Experienced [b]*BEING*[/b] that something.

To Experience what it is like to be something, One must also have Experience of [b]*NOT*[/b] being that something.

It is in the Experience of moving from being [b]*NOT*[/b] something, to [b]*BECOMING*[/b] that something, that we gain understanding of that something.

I could explain to you what it is like to be, say, a Special Forces soldier. And if my explanation was sufficiently correct, you would then [b]*KNOW*[/b] what it is like to be a Special Forces soldier.

But it wouldn't be until you became a Special Forces soldier yourself - Experience moving from [b]*NOT-BEING*[/b] a Special Forces soldier, to [b]*BEING*[/b] a Special Forces soldier - that you would fully understand and appreciate what it means to [b]*BE*[/b] a Special Forces soldier.
