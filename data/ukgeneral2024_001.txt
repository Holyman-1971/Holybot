I'm really hoping for a Hung Parliament.

Proportional Representation (of some form) is long overdue in the UK. I really hope that a result this week that doesn't produce a clear majority for a single party will hasten the move towards PR in the UK.

I hope the Reform Party does well.

Not because I support any of the Nationalist policies the Reform Party has, but because it seems quite clear that almost 20% of UK voters do support the Reform Party's policies. And the conceptual foundations of democracy require that Reform Party voters collective preference is reflected in the legislative body (House of Commons in the UK Parliament).

It seems abundantly clear that the UK Conservative Party is heading for the Exit, never to darken the doors of Downing Street again (and it is *WAY* past time that the Tories follow [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whigs_(British_political_party)]the Whigs[/url] into the Pages of History...).

But I'm not really sensing any appetite for the Labour Party, or Kier Starmer, come to that. Their poll numbers reflect a desire for "Anyone but the Conservatives!", but all Labour candidates and spokespeople have tried to remain pretty much silent about their policies, lest the UK's predominately right-wing press extrapolate details to substantiate their horrifying predictions of a return to the 1970's (particularly the 1978-1979 [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_of_Discontent]"Winter of Discontent"[/url]).

At the least, I really hope that if there is a surprise in the results come Friday morning, it will be that the Labour Party doesn't do as well as everyone is anticipating.

I'm voting for the Green Party.

Not because there is any chance of the parliamentary constituency I live in returning a Green Party MP; but because I want to make a contribution to the Green Party's share of the National Vote. And I do think the National Vote is going to be more critical this time around, than it has ever been before.

I have never had any firm political convictions of the "Right" and/or "Left" variety. I believe that Human Society is well past trying to organise and manage itself on ideological grounds.

The only social/political Idea I have is that it would be quite a good idea to try and save the Planet and prevent the extinction of the Human Race.

That idea is also the sole and driving Purpose of the Green Party.

So it gets my vote.

Also, [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Greens]the Green Party has a Global network[/url] of green political parties, partner networks, and other organisations associated with Green Politics. And that isn't something that any other political party can boast.

The Council on Foreign Relations has identified [url=https://www.cfr.org/backgrounder/how-green-party-success-reshaping-global-politics]"How Green-Party Success Is Reshaping Global Politics"[/url].

Now is really the Time to save our Planet for our children.

The only political and sociological idea *ALL* of us ought to be supporting right now, is the idea to save the Planet, and Humanity.

What happens in the UK tomorrow will move things along (not least of all by dissolving the current Conservative Party); but we all still have a great deal more to do.

And Proportional Representation will help a lot with that.
