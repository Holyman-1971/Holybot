I think it comes down to the unavoidable fact that so long as Israel maintains its 42-year long occupation of Palestine, and its 2-year long siege/blockade of the Gaza Strip, there is always going to be [b]*SOME*[/b] blowback from the Palestinians.

To imagine that somehow, some way, Israel could get [b]*ALL*[/b] Palestinians to just meekly endure the Occupation and Siege, is to try and imagine that Israelis would ever meekly endure Palestinian rocket attacks.

It just isn't going to happen.

Hamas could be destroyed as a functioning organisation, just as the PLO became the meek, quisling organisation that is today's Fatah. But Palestinian outrage would always find an outlet, somewhere, some way.

I don't say that by way of excusing or justifying Palestinian violence towards Israel; just by way of observing the unalterable fact that as long as Israel occupies Palestine, and treats Palestinians like cattle, it has to expect some form of militant response from certain sections of the Palestinian Community.

Just like the fact that in our own societies in the West, we accept that there is always going to be a certain background level of criminality and violence in our streets.

And when it comes to our deciding what response we as a Society can justify to street crime and violence, proportionality [b]*IS*[/b] relevant.

The Police do not launch artillery bombardments and tank invasions of the working-class neighbourhoods that likely play host to the criminals who occasionally assault the decent, honest, tax-paying middle-classes, every time a banker or a lawyer gets mugged - or even murdered.

There is, of course, a very small section of Society that would have no objection to that being done; but on the whole, the majority of us recognise that a certain degree of background threat is a Fact of Life, and we just have to learn to live with it.

We also recognise that essentially declaring War on the communities from where the greatest number of street criminals originate, would only make matters worse for ourselves. As the members of those communities who had never considered a life of crime, and who had never mugged or murdered anyone, might well reconsider their priorities, once they had been on the receiving end of the "War Against Muggers/Murderers".

Now, if there was a change of intensity to the background threat we face from street gangs and criminals - say if our chances of being mugged or murdered in the street went up from 1 in 100,000 to 1 in 1,000 - well then there might be a case for launching a far more determined and bellicose attempt to neutralise that threat.

But unless that were to happen, responding to the mugging or murder of a single member of the professional middle class, by slaughtering 500 working class people who might just happen to live near the person who possibly might could perhaps have maybe carried out the mugging or murder...

...Would be considered utterly inappropriate, irresponsible and overwhelmingly counter-productive.

And I don't see it is any different for Israel's response to the Palestinians in the Gaza Strip.

[b]*IF*[/b] the rockets fired by Hamas and other Palestinian militant groups were killing 3 or 4 Israelis a day; then perhaps Israel might be excused for responding by killing 1,300 Palestinians in the space of a couple of weeks.

But the fact is that in eight years of Palestinian rocket attacks, only a little fewer than two dozen Israelis have been murdered.

I'm not suggesting - and haven't suggested - that Israel do nothing.

But I am suggesting that if Israel would like to earn the respect and sympathy of the International Community (rather than be content solely with carrying the respect of certain homicidal and sociopathic Americans), then it should weigh carefully the measure of its response to Palestinian rocket attacks.

When all is said and done, when Israel has finished killing 1,300 Palestinians, seriously injuring 5,000 more, and traumatising and psychologically scarring hundreds of thousands of Palestinian children...

The rocket attacks against Israel will continue as though Israel had done nothing.

Except of course, the rocket attacks against Israel are more likely to continue as though Israel had just killed 1,300 Palestinians, and destroyed most of the Gaza Strip...
