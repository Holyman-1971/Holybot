Well, first things first, the idea and rationale behind the idea of the U.B.I. has gotten somewhat..."complexified" in this thread by its cross-pollination with SG's "Citizens' Pay Initiative". I think SG has already responded from the perspective of his Initiative, so I'll cover things from the U.B.I. perspective.

The primary rationale behind the U.B.I. is not the granting of financial subsistence to all and sundry, and/or affording them the opportunity to work less, or give up work altogether. That may be a consequence where some people are concerned, but it is a secondary effect.

The main justification for supplying a guaranteed basic income to everyone is Economic, not Social.

Economies do not thrive and prosper because everybody is working their pants off; Economic growth is fuelled by Economic Activity, not Labour Productivity.

Economic Activity is measured by the circulation of money within an Economy. Entities - individual or organisational - that hoard cash retard or prevent its circulation. If money does not circulate, the Economy stagnates.

Money is not a thing, in and of itself; it is (variously) a [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medium_of_exchange]medium of exchange[/url], a [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_of_account]unit of account[/url], and/or a [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Store_of_value]store of value[/url]. What it is not, is something of intrinsic or inherent value, in and of itself.

When SG talks about a $40k p.a. figure for his C.P.I., I suspect he is fixing (or estimating) that figure primarily based on what kind of a lifestyle such an income would afford him, right now, in his present situation. It is not then the raw "Dollar Amount" that he is concerned with, but Lifestyle Augmentation!

The figure (of approximately $12k p.a. for an average U.S. Citizen) cited for the Universal Basic Income is based on an assessment of the cost of the basic necessities of life (just above poverty levels)  are today, generally, in the U.S. It is further determined by reference to what the various State and Federal Government departments involved are spending right now... On keeping the recipients of Social Welfare from dying in the streets, right now...

So the principle behind the U.B.I. is not a raw dollar figure, but the idea that money needs to move more rapidly and more widely within the Economy; and the money that is *CURRENTLY* being spent on ensuring Citizens have the basic minimum required to survive, will move more rapidly and more widely if it is given directly to those Citizens, rather than having to ooze its way through layers of Government Bureaucracy.

And the rationale for making it Universal (i.e. every Citizen gets it, regardless of their circumstances) is how you eliminate that bureaucracy.

This is really key to differentiating between the Universal Basic Income, and SG's idea for a Citizen's Pay Initiative.

The point is that right now, if Alaskan_Viking, or you, or anyone else wants to give up working, you can.

One way or another, you will be able to avail yourself of some form of Welfare Assistance to ensure that you and your family do not die of poverty. Sure, there will be a lot of red tape to wade through; you'll be asked to justify why you have decided to no longer work; you'll have to convince the bureaucrats that there are good reasons why you won't work...

But if nothing else, you'll be able to ultimately claim Mental Unfitness to Work... Because if you just stop... Everything... Then sooner or later, Social Welfare will have to kick in. Because as disinclined as your Society is to look after the workshy and feckless; it is also disinclined to have the World think of it as some uncivilised place where poor people are just left to die in the streets.

So eventually, your Society - your State and Federal Government - will start picking up the tab to provide you with a meagre existence.

And it will be meagre.

It will just be the bare essentials.

Which you can start applying for right now, if you want to live a meagre existence on the bare essentials.

Do you want to live a meagre existence, with only the bare essentials to keep you going?

You probably don't.

Things wouldn't be any different with a Universal Basic Income (or Friedman's "Negative Income Tax").

You just won't have to wade through the Red Tape to first apply and then maintain the supply of a basic welfare income.

But you'll still be living the same meagre existence.

Just that the money that you will need to fund that meagre existence will come to you, no questions asked; and you'll spend it immediately, because you'll have to.

So overall, the money will be circulating around the Economy that much faster.

Which is a good thing for the Economy.

Because the faster that money moves within an Economy, the more growth it will stimulate.

Just as you don't want billionaires hoarding cash and preventing its circulation; nor do you want Government Bureaucracies (which have an additional cost overhead to boot) doing it either.

That's the idea behind the Universal Basic Income.

I think SG's C.P.I. is more about Personal Wish Fulfilment.
