More Than Two Ways to Cast a Vote

I don't know exactly what is going to happen in tomorrow's U.S. Presidential Election; though it does look like Senator Obama and the Democratic Party are going to emerge victorious, to one degree or another.

But from where I'm sitting, it strikes me that if Obama does get the keys to the White House, it won't be because he will enact any significant or radical changes, in either Domestic or Foreign Policy. Certainly he hasn't suggested or promised any on the Campaign Trail. Rather, he has just stuck as closely to the most Centrist and least objectionable manifesto possible, whilst watching the GOP and its candidate implode.

Which brings me to the purpose of this poll. And that is to try and get an understanding of just why it is people might, would or will vote for Obama and not McCain.

Is it that you support Barack Obama's policies; or that you reject the continuity of the disastrous Bush Administration, represented by John McCain?

Some of you of course, can't vote tomorrow, because you're not American citizens, and can only endure the consequences of U.S. Government actions, without being able to democratically affect them. But WTF, we're All One Tribe these days, whether we care to admit it or not; and right now, Humanity's Capital Centre is somewhere along the Eastern seaboard of the United States of America.

So vote away.
