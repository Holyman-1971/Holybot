So practically speaking, which will be the more dominant effect: The eventual fall in house prices or the devaluation of the US dollar?

The devaluation of the dollar will have (is having) a much more immediate impact on consumer price indices.

Should the dollar go into free-fall, the U.S. Trade Deficit ($800 billion - and entirely the responsibility of the U.S. Government) will mean that most every day imported consumer items, including gas, will become prohibitively expensive for U.S. Consumers.

Add in the U.S. Current Account deficit, and the Budget deficit, and it is very difficult to see how the U.S. Dollar will survive as a viable currency.

In otherwords, am I better off going ahead and overpaying for a house now and letting inflation catch up, or waiting it out until home prices fall?

Wait it out until home prices fall.

You shouldn't have to wait more than about 12 - 18 months before prices begin to bottom out, and by that point, you should be able to buy at least 2 or 3 similar properties, for the price that you would pay for just one of those properties now.
