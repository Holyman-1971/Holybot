Yup, there's plenty available... And I could get one of my guys to put something together in short order, and I'd be able to host it no problem.

However...

The most important foundation for an On-line Community is a sense of Balance.

What works really well about our Community is that it is hosted by a far-off and distant commercial organisation, who aren't too fussed about exactly what takes place in terms of content.

The Moderators do a spot-on job - the best I've ever seen - of moderating with a very light touch; but it is important nevertheless that there is [b]*SOME*[/b] moderation. A completely unmoderated Forum would be a recipe for disaster. There needs to be at least a sense of [b]*SOME*[/b] restraint and control.

Now because there is a sense that the rules are laid down by IGN/PlanetForums, and moderated by Spandex & Co., when moderation takes place, it isn't the moderators who are directly and personally blamed, but rather the commercial organisation whose resources provide the Forum facility.

If our Forum was hosted by ourselves, or by just one or two of us, then requirements for moderation, and every attempt to moderate, would be blamed on the Hoster/Poster. This absolutely would sow the seeds for dissent, fraction and ultimately schism.

For instance, though I could easily provide and host our Forum facilities, I'm one of the most contentious and provocative posters around here. If it was felt by my respondents that they couldn't really let rip at me, because I might decide to subjectively apply the Ban-Hammer, it really would unsettle the Balance of our Community.

For example, there's no way I would suffer SomeGuy to continue to litter the place with his sophomoric drivel, not if I was Master-Overlord-Supreme-Keeper-of-the-Forum. But the fact of the matter is that it is [b]*NOT*[/b] my place to decide whether SomeGuy stays or goes: that is a decision that [b]*IF*[/b] it is made by anyone, must be made by the Community as a Whole.

Though it pains me to say it, annoying, irritating and woefully naive though SomeGuy is, he does seem to have been accepted as a "Feature" of our Community. If the Community as a Whole does not reject him; then it is not the place of any single member - even a Moderator - to decide to eject him.

Equally, though this Forum is pretty relaxed and robust when it comes to the language and discourse in use, there are certain activities that [b]*ARE*[/b] still unacceptable.

"Svetlana" is a cool thread, precisely because it has to stay within the realms of at least a modicum of decency. Try to imagine a "Svetlana" thread populated entirely with hardcore pornographic images, and you'd see how it would negatively affect the tone and dynamic of the Forum.

Same goes with linking out to sites with unacceptable and wholly NSFW content. It's only the constraints imposed by IGN/Planet Battlefield that stops Hoff putting up links to gay pornography, every time he gets a strop on.

So all in all Bunny, whilst setting up our own Forum facilities would seem the sensible thing to do, I really do think that it would mark the end of our Community.
