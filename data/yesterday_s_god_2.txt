Oh, okay, well, just to be clear, the two "camps" I'm talking about are Science/Rationalism and Spirituality, *NOT* Religion.

I have zero belief in an anthropomorphic deity or deities. I can't absolutely state that no single, supernatural patriarch or polytheistic pantheon exists, since apparently, according to their various liturgies, I might be under the spell of or in thrall to some "devil" or "evil spirit" that has completely twisted my powers of perception and understanding....

To me, Spirituality is quite different to Religion. The Human Spirit is the ineffable quality that we are all aware of, but don't seem to be able to fully quantify or qualify. But that as a Species we continually strive to do so, is, as far as I can tell, pretty much what keeps us all going.

I think that there is a space where Science and Spirituality converge, but it isn't (and necessarily *CAN'T* be) Religion: it's Philosophy. And I think Philosophy - literally: The Love of Wisdom - has pretty much been side-lined because of the Human War between Science and Religion.
