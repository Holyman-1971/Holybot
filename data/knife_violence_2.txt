That wasn't quite my point.

I don't think we can take weapons away, because human beings, particularly children, are too ingeneous: they will always find something to use as a weapon.

It's not practical to ban all knives in the UK, and it's certainly not practical to ban all guns in the US.

My point wasn't that we try to keep weapons away from our children; but that we try to keep our children away from weapons.

There's a subtle and important distinction there; and it's mostly about who has to do the hard work.

To keep weapons away from our children, we have to look to our governments and politicians; and they, frankly, couldn't give a fuck about our kids.

To keep our children away from weapons requires us as parents to act, not sit back and wait for our government to come up with a solution.

It means spending more time with our children, and being more positive, supportive and caring. It's the easiest thing in the World to look after a little bundle of soft, squidgy cutsieness, when our kids are in their single-digit years; but once they hit double-figures...

Well, that's where they become this sort of strange representative of an alien culture, of which we are inherently suspicious and fearful. So we just shout and scream at them, and now that they're old enough, give them a solid smack, clip 'round the ear, or kick up the arse.

What we find it almost impossible to do, as parents, is to find the time and patience to help our children through adolescence; and help them navigate through that most challenging of waters: teenage peer-pressure.

A sign of a truly civilised Society would be one where you could lead a teenage male into a room, show him a table with a semi-automatic pistol, a guitar, a pen, and a computer on it, and know with absolute confidence that he will [b]*NOT*[/b] pick up the gun.

That he will not even consider picking up the gun.

Like I say, it isn't about keeping weapons away from our children; it's about keeping our children away from even the [b]*IDEA*[/b] of needing to use weapons for any reason other than controlled sports.
