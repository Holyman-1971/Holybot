Now that's quite an emotive statement, isn't it? It seems to suggest that Sharia Law is going to become a part of English jurisprudence. And we all know that Sharia Law is bad, mm'kay.

If we take the headline at face value, it must necessarily provoke an emotional and possibly extreme response in the reader, as they "...wonder what the World is coming to!"

[i]"To ask the Secretary of State for Justice whether there are any courts in England and Wales which incorporate elements of Sharia law in their work; and if he will make a statement."[/i]

The immediate answer Mr. Streeter received from Bridget Prentice, MP, junior minister in the Justice Ministry was:

[i]"The legal system that operates the courts in England and Wales does not incorporate any elements of Sharia Law."[/i]
