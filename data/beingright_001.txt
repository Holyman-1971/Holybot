What does it mean to be "Right"?

To save our Pedants from wasting Time or Energy:

[quote][b]Adjective:[/b]

morally good, justified or acceptable - [i]"I hope we're doing the right thing"[/i]

true or correct as a fact - [i]"I'm not sure I know the right answer"[/i]

[b]Adverb:[/b]

to the furthest or most complete extent or degree (used for emphasis) - [i]"The car spun right off the track"[/i]

correctly - [i]"He had guessed right"[/i]

[b]Noun:[/b]

that which is morally correct, just, or honourable - [i]"She doesn't understand the difference between right and wrong"[/i]

a moral or legal entitlement to have or do something - [i]"She had every right to be angry"[/i]

[b]Verb:[/b]

restore to a normal or upright position - [i]"We righted the capsized dinghy"[/i]

restore to a normal or correct state - [i]"Righting the economy demanded major cuts in defence spending"[/i][/quote] - [url= https://languages.oup.com/google-dictionary-en/]Oxford Languages[/url]

Looking at those definitions, to my mind, there is only one definition that accurately answers the question, "What does it mean to *BE* right?".

Which means that the answer can only be a verb.

I'll combine those two definitions into one:

To restore to a normal, upright or correct position or state.

So:

[b]Individually and Collectively as Humans Being, what is our normal, upright or correct, position or state?[/b]

Because we can't hope to ever Be what we are unable to define and Visualise.
