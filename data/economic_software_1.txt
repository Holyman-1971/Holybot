This simply is not a political issue; it is a practical social issue about the way that Human civilisation is constructed and operated.

And it is no more an ideological issue than it is a moral or religious issue.

We human beings leave Economic matters in the hands of politicians and other social leaders at our peril.

Let's pick up on what EvilAirWolfy posted:

[quote=EvilAirWolfy][color=green]Whether you be a socialist/capitalist or whatever, the money does not matter. It's worth is plucked out of thin air. A group of 3 could create a unit of trade between themselves.

That's it.

That's it a [b]*LOT*[/b] more succinctly than I'm about to describe it. That's the essence of Human Economics. It is the way that the "sweat from our backs" - our personal efforts - converts the resources that we have at our disposal, into something that we or others, need or desire.

Everything else related to Economics stems from this basic paradigm.

So how did it get to be so fucking complicated?

How did it get so that when the CEO's and Chairmen of the so-called "foundations" of our Capitalist systems - the Banks - are hauled before our public representatives, they are forced to confess that they have no fucking clue as to how the systems that got us into this mess work?

See, you don't have to be embarrassed to admit that you don't understand Economics, because the Truth is that [b]*NO-ONE*[/b] understands how Economics works.

But it's like the old joke about the two wildlife cameramen approaching the pride of hungry lions. One of the cameramen is wearing fast running shoes, and the other guy scoffs that this is pointless, because he'll never be able to outrun a lion. And the cameraman wearing the running shoes replies that he doesn't need to outrun the lions, he just needs to outrun the other cameraman.

To be financially successful, you don't have to understand completely how Economics works: you just have to understand it better than most.

And bizarrely, given that it has a greater impact on all of our lives than politics, religion or celebrity culture, most of us seem to have switched completely off from all regard or interest about how Economics functions and dominates our Human lives. We have allowed ourselves to be convinced that Economics is - must [b]*NEED*[/b] to be - impossibly complex and impenetrable, and that we have far more pressing needs that must be addressed in our lives (like which politician, religion or celebrity we are going to believe in...).

And this abject resignation has given the "Masters of the Universe" who dominate the Economic Savannah, free reign to run rampant in a way that has [b]*ALWAYS*[/b] adversely affected our lives.

And now, as we see those people we always believed (hoped?) knew what they were doing, scratching their heads, wondering where it all went wrong, we feel even more helpless than usual, even as we are being more adversely affected than usual. And all we feel we can do, is to point our fingers at those we thought we had entrusted our livelihoods to, and sit back and hope that they will come up with an answer to the problems that [b]*THEY*[/b] created...

And that's a bit silly really.

Much better to go back to basics, ask the basic questions, and recognise that those who have the most to lose, are unlikely to come up with the basic answers to the basic, fundamental problems.

It's like EvilAirWolfy says, "... the money does not matter. It's worth is plucked out of thin air."

The whole Global Economic System is an abstract; it is a notional construct that exists solely in the minds of Human Beings.

It's basically Software; but software that runs on organic, sentient computers called the Human Brain.

And the Software that we are using - that we have been using for more than 300 years - is broken; it is corrupt; it is bugged beyond recovery.

It has crashed.

Capitalism is a "Software" application that has collapsed - as it always had to, eventually - under the weight of its own systemic flaw: Debt.

Communism is - or was - an Economic Software package, written using the Capitalism code framework, just restructured to try and redistribute some of the output. It's main problem, like Socialism, is that it was too monolithic in design, too focused on the Centre, and hence prone to a (very big) single point of failure (at the Centre).

The new Economic Software we have to create, must be more flexible, more resilient, more adaptable, less dependent on core critical structures, and more able to incorporate on-going innovation.

In short, we need Open Source, Peer-to-Peer Economic Software, to replace the failed, proprietary, Client-Server Software that has dominated our lives for the past 3 centuries.

If we were going to sit down and write the code for a Virtual Economy for a Virtual Society, we just would not come up with the same kind of inherently unstable, inherently flawed and inherently inequitable system that Capitalism is. Not because it would be morally or ideologically wrong to do so, but simply because it would be rejected by the denizens of that Virtual Society we were trying to support and nurture. And they'd reject it not for moral or ideological reasons, but for practical reasons: it simply wouldn't work.

Meanwhile, back in the [b]"Real World" (tm)[/b], we blithely and dumbly accept the continuation of our actually flawed Economic Systems, whilst waiting on tender hooks for the "Developers" - the Bankers and Financiers, and the politicians who run cover for them - to deliver a Software Patch that might just hold the System together long enough for them to make good their escape from any responsibility for the mess they continue to create and sustain.

The Whole System needs to be redesigned and rebuilt from scratch, working from the very basic principle of a requirement for an Economic System that can support rather than dominate our Collective Existence.

And it really doesn't take rocket scientists, Nobel Laureate Economists, or "Masters of the Universe" to do that. Those people are guaranteed to lose out with any move away from their proprietary systems. If we wait for them to come up with a solution, we'll be waiting a very long time.

Probably more Time than we have available to us.
