Perhaps using the exactly the same kind of tactics as SWAT teams use even in the fiercest neighbourhoods in the U.S. would not be effective. But there are intermediate degrees between the American SWAT approach, and all-out air, artillery and armoured bombardment.

The Elephant in the Room here is the expressed acknowledgement by Israel that the tactics it has used recently in Gaza are primarily intended to restore the deterrent effect of Israel's Armed Forces, which has been in steady decline for some years, especially since its invasion of Lebanon in 2006.

Fact is that it is the Israeli military's modus operandi to attack civilian neighbourhoods with overwhelming and indiscriminate force, in order to ultimately deter the Palestinian population (those who survive anyway) from its support of Hamas.

The supporting propaganda posits that Hamas is [b]*DIRECTLY*[/b] responsible for all the death and destruction caused by Israel; and the Israelis bear no responsibility for doing only what they were compelled to do.

On the one hand Israel and its supporters cite Hamas' actions as illegal and terroristic; but on the other hand Israel provides a military response.

If what Hamas' militants are doing is illegal, a crime, then the response must be a Police response, targeting [b]*ONLY*[/b] the criminals responsible for the crime in question, and keeping to an absolute minimum the number of innocent civilians caught in the crossfire.

But by responding with overwhelming (and hopefully deterring) military force, Israel elevates the original acts of Hamas' militants to the status of an Act of War.

And by killing Palestinian civilians, legitimises Hamas' killing of Israeli civilians.

A purposefully minimalist response, solely designed to neutralise the Hamas threat, using methods clearly intended to minimise civilian casualties, would not incur the approbation of the Rest of the World, and would not act to create further rage and hostility towards Israel from the radicalised Palestinian population of Gaza.

Though maybe that last is precisely the reason [b]*WHY*[/b] Israel uses the methods it does.
