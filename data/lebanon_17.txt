Wipe your mental slate clean Brownshirts, forget all the rights and wrongs of the Conflict for a moment, and consider: what possible fucking chance of victory does Israel have?

And a couple of supplementals to consider, if you can pluck up the courage to respond:

With the possible exception of Israel, and Zionist Terror Groups in the British Palestinian Mandate: can you give any examples of a situation where extremists using terroristic methods, ever defeated a nation-state, and permanently replaced its government?

And, can you give any examples of a situation where a conventional, Government controlled military force ever succeeded in completely wiping out an entire militant organisation?

And whilst you might be considering your response, consider also how Irish Republican terrorism was ended by negotiations with the British Government. Consider how the Spanish Government engaged with the Basque Separatist movement. How the Sri Lankan Government is trying to negotiate with the Tamil Tigers.

Consider also the Indian Government's response to the recent Mumbai bombings. Did it invade or bomb Pakistan?

And finally, consider the 40-odd UN Security Council Resolutions against Israel automatically vetoed by the United States. Consider the 60 or so, non-binding General Assembly Resolutions that Israel continues to ignore. Consider the fact that despite Israel having one of the top 25 World Economies, it remains the number one recipient of US financial and military aid - the US can't work fast enough to get more munitions over to Israel, for use against Lebanese civilians, in direct contravention of US Law.

And consider that over the 60 years of the Arab-Israeli Conflict, Israel has killed disproportionately more Arabs, than Arabs have killed Israelis.

Consider that both parties to the Camp David talks agreed that negotiations based on UNSC Resolutions 242 and 338 were the only way forward; and that only Israel is in a position to implement and enforce those resolutions - whenever it chooses.

And finally, consider that Israel and the United States' definition of "Terrorism", is any violence whatosever against Israel and the United States, even violence against military targets.

And maybe, just maybe, if you can answer those questions, and consider those points honestly, you might understand why one-sided, unequivocal support for Israel, is absolutely and very, very obviously not the way to go about resolving the whole Conflict, and bring Peace to the region.
