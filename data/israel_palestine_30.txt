[b][i]January 26th, 2009[/i]

Israel and Hamas still disagree on ceasefire[/b]

Israel and Hamas have yet to reach agreement on a long-term ceasefire in the Gaza Strip. The Palestinian movement has proposed a one-year ceasefire to Egyptian mediators, but Israel wants the ceasefire to last at least 18 months, which in turn has been rejected by Hamas. The parties are also diametrically opposed on the issue of border crossings. Israel wants to re-open only a limited number of crossings, while Hamas insists on all crossings being re-opened so aid can be brought into the Gaza Strip unhindered.
