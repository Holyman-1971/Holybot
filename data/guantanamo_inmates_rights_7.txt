[quote=Foota21178388][color=red]Why doesn't the Geneva Convention give Unlawful/Illegal Combatant's the same rights as POW's?[/color][/quote]

The Geneva Conventions [b]*DO*[/b] give unlawful combatants the same rights as POW's Foota.

Article 5 of the 3rd Geneva Convention states that the status of a detainee may be determined by a "competent tribunal"; until such time, he is to be treated as a prisoner of war.

http://www.abanet.org/irr/hr/winter03/unlawful.html

Under the 4th Geneva Convention, someone who is determined by a "competent tribunal" to be an unlawful combatant, retains rights and privileges such that he must be "treated with humanity and, in case of trial, shall not be deprived of the rights of fair and regular trial".

http://www.icrc.org/Web/eng/siteeng0.nsf/htmlall/5LPHBV/%24File/irrc_849_Dorman.pdf
