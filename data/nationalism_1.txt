Politics and the conditions of the world today are way too complicated to just say, Defend yourselves world.

At the end of World War Two, the United States took the lead in recognising that the old Westphalian system of inviolable sovereign integrity, supported by mutual defence pacts and alliances, was precisely what led to the destructive wars of the first half of the 20th Century (as well as the European Wars of the 18th and 19th Centuries).

It was because of this that the United States took the initiative to found the United Nations. The idea behind the United Nations was that disputes between nations could and would be settled within the General Assembly, mediated by the international community.

Whilst the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation and the Warsaw Pact existed subsequent to the founding of the United Nations, these two �Alliances� were recognised as being simply the forward force-projection of the two opposed Super-Powers, the United States and the Soviet Union. Spain could no more influence or control NATO policy than Bulgaria could control the Soviet military.

The idea of the United Nations then was a fairly obvious one. Humanity has continued to evolve its sense of collective identity, as much as it had developed the technical and cultural underpinnings of that identity. Just as the original 13 colonies of America had formed the United States, and the four countries of the British Isles came together under the Union of the United Kingdom, so too does the expansion of international commerce, communications and transport logically lead to the idea of a single, Global identity.
