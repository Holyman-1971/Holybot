Masters of our Own Dominion, we are still subject to the whims of Nature, we can not exert complete control over the events in our lives. This experience requires us to consider the possibility of a �Greater Power� (�Greater� than Human Power at any rate), and for some humans that is an invitation to scientific exploration, to others an invitation to religious fidelity, and perhaps to some, a matter of supreme irrelevance.

Science is, philosophically speaking, and to all intents and purposes, engaged in a process of trying to prove that God does not exist. And science uses potentially falsifiable theories and proven scientific laws to support this attempt to prove a negative. Religion on the other hand, is engaged in an equally dogmatic process of continual assertion that God does and *must* exist. Religion uses testimony and doctrine to assert the validity of this positive.

Essentially though, science and religion are engaged in a �You say po-ta-toe, I say po-tat-oe� process.

Science states that the Entirety of Existence is governed by fathomable rules and laws, all of which are, ultimately, within the grasp of the human intellect. The �Greater Power� that influences and affects human life then, is a deeply complex system of energy interchange and interplay, acting on deterministic and/or chaotic principles. Science even expresses the theory that there is a �Theory of Everything�, or ToE. Somewhere down the line, human faculty and experience will reach a state where everything in the Universe, and everything that happens in the Universe, will be explainable along rational, demonstrable, empirical lines.

The Theory of Everything will provide the basis for understanding what the �Greater Power� really is, and of course, how it can be controlled by humanity.

Theoretically speaking.

Religion states that the Entirety of Existence is governed by the unfathomable plans and desires of a Supreme Being. This Supreme Being, God, is implicitly omniscient, omnipotent and omnipresent. In other words, God is a deeply complex system of energy interchange, acting on deterministic and chaotic principles, governing every aspect of everything that happens in the Universe. Humanity inherently lacks the faculty and capacity to ever comprehend the totality of God�s awesome power, and so it shouldn�t even bother to try. Rather humanity should just sit back, take received wisdom from God on faith, and cheerfully co-operate with whatever God has in store for them.

God *is* the basis of understanding. And humanity can never control God; God controls humanity.

Theologically speaking.

According to scientific theory as it stands today, the Big Bang created both space and time. Before the Big Bang, there was no space, and there was no time: there was no �before�. Therefore, whilst we can theoretically state that it has been around fifteen billion years since the Big Bang commenced; we are also obliged to state that the Big Bang itself occurred, very literally, in no time at all. The Big Bang has happened, is happening and will happen. Time is a by-product of the Big Bang, the creation of the Universe; the Big Bang was not an event *within* space-time; but is rather the event that *created* space-time. The Universe itself is not constrained by time. Without the Big Bang, without the Universe, there would be no space, and there would be no time.

According to most religious doctrine, as it has stood for quite some time, it was God that created both space and time. Before God, there was no space, and there was no time. Theologically speaking, before God there was no �before�. God simply Is; and everything that flows from God, including space, time, and the individual entities that exist within the space-time created by God, are all by-products of God�s Existence. God is not constrained by or subject to time; without God there would be no time.

Whether you choose to believe or not believe in science or religion, is simply a matter of predisposition, indoctrination and personal experience.

Whether you choose to believe or not in space or time, is simply a matter of how securely you need to anchor your identity within a larger framework than Self.
