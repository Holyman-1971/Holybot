To really appreciate "Infinity War/Endgame", you do have to have watched most/all of the preceding films. I wouldn't expect anyone to share my view of it being the Greatest Film of All Time, unless they had watched (and enjoyed most of) the films leading up to it. There are some of the MCU films that aren't all that (see below), but the trick is to at least watch the origin films for each of the Avengers, and of course, the 3 previous Avengers films ("Captain America: Civil War" = Avengers 2.5).

Think of the whole MCU Canon as a series. You need to watch every episode to get the full effect.

For your viewing pleasure then Wunder, try watching them in the following order (and if you and Mrs. Wunder have a Disney+ subscription, you can watch them *ALL* at your leisure):

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0458339/]Captain America: The First Avenger[/url] - Released 2011 - 5/5[/size]

If you haven't seen this one yet Wunder, I highly recommend you start with it. Chronologically (in the MCU Timeline) it is the first film, and it is set in World War Two, with Steve Rogers (aka Captain America) and his [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Howling_Commandos]Howling Commandos[/url] going up against the [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Skull]Red Skull[/url]. Red Skull is the Nazi who took the cult of [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Hive]Hive/Alveus[/url] to a whole new level by founding [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/HYDRA]HYDRA[/url], and is performed by the outstanding [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0915989/]Hugo Weaving[/url] (aka Agent Smith, Elrond, and V).

It is important to watch this film because it is the beginning of [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0262635/]Chris Evans'[/url] Captain America story arc that completes so movingly in "Endgame"; and because we get to meet "Bucky" Barnes for the first time (who goes on to become the [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Winter_Soldier]Winter Soldier[/url]. It is also a really good standalone movie, especially if you're into all the commando stuff.  :D

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt4154664/]Captain Marvel[/url] - Released 2019 - 4/5[/size]

...Is the next movie chronologically in the MCU Timeline. It introduces us to [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Captain_Marvel]Captain Marvel[/url], aka Carol Danvers, played by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0488953/]Brie Larson[/url]. And it is also the first time (in the MCU Chronology) that we meet Samuel L. Jackson's [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Nick_Fury]Nick Fury[/url], the character who will go on to actually assemble the Avengers.

Important to watch in order to get an understanding of why Captain Marvel is just about the most powerful individual in the MCU.

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0371746/]Iron Man[/url] - Released 2008 - 5/5[/size]

This is the MCU Movie that kicked it all off and introduced the World to [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Iron_Man]Tony Stark/Iron Man[/url], portrayed by the incomparable [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000375/]Robert Downey, Jr.[/url]. Another one I think you'll enjoy Wunder, if you haven't seen it already...

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1228705/]Iron Man 2[/url] - Released 2010 - 4/5[/size]

Following on from the previous film, this one has [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000620/]Mickey Rourke[/url] playing the villain, and also introduces [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0424060/]Scarlett Johansson[/url] as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Widow]Natasha Romanoff, aka Black Widow[/url] (we'll be seeing a *LOT* more of Black Widow as the MCU Timeline progresses. It is also the first time out for [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000332/]Don Cheadle[/url] as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/War_Machine]War Machine[/url] - a good friend of Tony Stark's and possessor of a more military-grade Iron Man suit.

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0800080/]The Incredible Hulk[/url] - Released 2008 - 3/5[/size]

Released at more or less the same time as the original "Iron Man" movie, this one has [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001570/]Edward Norton[/url] playing [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Hulk]Bruce Banner/Hulk[/url]; [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000619/]Tim Roth[/url] as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Abomination]Abomination/Emil Blonsky[/url]; [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000239/]Liv Tyler[/url] as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Betty_Ross]Betty Ross[/url], Bruce Banner's girlfriend, and daughter of [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Hulk]General Thaddeus "Thunderbolt" Ross[/url], played here (and throughout the MCU until he died in 2022) by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000458/]William Hurt[/url].

It doesn't feel as foundational a movie as "Iron Man" (and Hulk hasn't had a standalone movie since), but it *DOES* have a cameo from [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0002073/]Lou Ferrigno[/url], who played the Hulk in the [url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0077031/]TV series we all watched and loved[/url].  8-)

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0800369/]Thor[/url] - Released 2011 - 5/5[/size]

Oh, this is the one that I was really waiting for! [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Thor]Thor[/url] was always my favourite character in the comics and I think [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000110/]Kenneth Branagh[/url] did an incredible job directing Thor's origin movie.

Also introduces [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000164/]Anthony Hopkins[/url] as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Odin]Odin[/url], Thor's dad, and [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000204/]Natalie Portman[/url] as Thor's Earth-based girlfriend, [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Mighty_Thor]Jane Foster[/url].

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0848228/]The Avengers[/url] - Released 2012 - 5/5[/size]

Oh, now we're talking.  8-)

Called "Avengers Assemble" in the UK (on account of [url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0118661/]"The Avengers"[/url] title already having been used for the barely-noticed movie version of the British TV series of the same name). The first movie that brought together all of the Avengers we have met so far, including [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0719637/]Jeremy Renner[/url] as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Hawkeye]Hawkeye[/url], who we first saw in the "Thor" movie.

I'm sure you've seen it, so I don't need to tell you how good it is.  8-)

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1981115/]Thor: Dark World[/url] - Released 2013 - 4/5[/size]

...Doesn't have quite the same impact as the original "Thor" movie, but does have an important storyline around one of the Infinity Stones. It also lays important foundations for the relationship between Thor and his (adopted) brother, [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Loki]Loki[/url], played (as I'm sure you know) by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1089991/]Tom Hiddleston[/url].

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1300854/]Iron Man 3[/url] - Released 2013 - 4/5[/size]

This one has Tony Stark suffering from PTSD, following his battle with Loki and his minions in "Avengers Assemble". It also has [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001426/]Ben Kingsley[/url] giving a very funny performance as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Trevor_Slattery]Trevor Slattery[/url], aka [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Xu_Wenwu]Mandarin[/url].

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1843866/]Captain America: The Winter Soldier[/url] - Released 2014 - 5/5[/size]

Steve Rogers/Captain America learns what happened to his best mate, Bucky Barnes. Also exposes HYDRA's infiltration of [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/S.H.I.E.L.D.]S.H.I.E.L.D.[/url] and has a major Washington, D.C.-set battle as its climax.

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2015381/]Guardians of the Galaxy[/url] - Released 2014 - 5/5[/size]

This one blew everyone away, mostly because it was unexpected. [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Galaxy]Guardians of the Galaxy[/url] were a little-known team of Marvel characters, led by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0695435/]Chris Pratt's[/url] [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Star-Lord]Star-Lord[/url]. It has a cybernetically-augmented racoon called [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Rocket_Raccoon]Rocket[/url], voiced by the outrageously-talented [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0177896/]Bradley Cooper[/url], a talking-tree called [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Groot]Groot[/url], voiced by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0004874/]Vin Diesel[/url], and [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Drax_the_Destroyer]Drax the Destroyer[/url], portrayed by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1176985/]Dave Bautista[/url], in a role he was born to play.

It also introduces us for the first time to [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Gamora]Gamora[/url], played by the beautiful [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0757855/]Zoe Saldaña[/url], who just-so happens to be the daughter (adopted) of [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Thanos]Thanos[/url], the mad Titan, played by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000982/]Josh Brolin[/url].

Also in this film:

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1195855/]Lee Pace[/url] plays the baddie, [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Ronan_the_Accuser]Ronan the Accuser[/url] (who is hunting another one of those Infinity Stones).

Ex-Eastenders actor [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm2394794/]Karen Gillan[/url] as Gamora's (also adopted) sister, [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Nebula]Nebula[/url].

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0740264/]Michael Rooker[/url] plays [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Yondu]Yondu Udonta[/url], leader of an exiled faction of the [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Ravagers]Ravagers[/url], and Star-Lord's/Peter Quill's adoptive father.

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0005023/]Djimon Hounsou[/url] plays [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Korath_the_Pursuer]Korath the Pursuer[/url], Ronan the Accuser's lead henchman.

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000335/]Glenn Close[/url] plays [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Irani_Rael]Nova Prime[/url], commander of the [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Nova_Corps]Nova Corps[/url].

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000604/]John C. Reilley[/url] gives a charmingly-witty performance as a member of the Nova Corps.

And [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001125/]Benicio Del Toro[/url] plays [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Collector]The Collector[/url].

Really great cast, and outstanding direction from [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0348181/]James Gunn[/url].

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3896198/]Guardians of the Galaxy: Vol. 2[/url] - Released 2017 - 4/5[/size]

Another hit from James Gunn & Co.

Two main MCU storylines from this film are about Star-Lord discovering who his actual father is, [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Ego]Ego, the Living Planet[/url], portrayed by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000621/]Kurt Russell[/url]; and the relationship between Gamora and Nebula.

Also has an appearance from [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000230/]Sylvester Stallone[/url] as the leader of another Ravager faction; and we meet [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Mantis]Mantis[/url] for the first time, played by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm2962353/]Pom Klementieff[/url]. Post-credit scene also teases the arrival of [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Adam_Warlock]Adam Warlock[/url], who in the comics was created to deal with Thanos and is like, super-super-powerful...

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2395427/]Avengers: Age of Ultron[/url] - Released 2015 - 5/5[/size]

Second outing for the Avengers as a unit, [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000652/]James Spader[/url] voices [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Ultron]Ultron[/url], the AI-entity that Tony Stark and Bruce Banner create in the hope of providing a "suit of armour" around the World.

This film also sees the birth of [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Vision]Vision[/url], played by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0079273/]Paul Bettany[/url], who is a serious force (for the good). And we also meet [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0647634/]Elizabeth Olsen's[/url] [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Scarlet_Witch]Scarlet Witch[/url], who will become one of the most powerful Marvel characters (up there with Captain Marvel and Adam Warlock).

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0478970/]Ant-Man[/url] - Released 2015 - 4/5[/size]

Not many people know that [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Ant-Man]Ant-Man[/url] was one of the founding members of The Avengers in the Marvel comics, along with Iron Man, Thor, and Hulk (and their first mission together was the rescue of Captain America from the ice he'd been trapped in since World War Two).

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000140/]Michael Douglas[/url] plays *THAT* Ant-Man, aka [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Hank_Pym]Hank Pym[/url], and [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0748620/]Paul Rudd[/url] plays Scott Laing, who (one way and another) takes on the mantle of Ant-Man. The incredibly beautiful [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1431940/]Evangeline Lilly[/url] plays Hank's daughter, Hope van Dyne, who will eventually become the [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Wasp]Wasp[/url] and Ant-Man's girlfriend.

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3498820/]Captain America: Civil War[/url] - Released 2016 - 5/5[/size]

So this movie is basically Avengers 2.5, in that it brings pretty much everyone together (except Thor, Hulk, and the Guardians of the Galaxy); and it introduces [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm4043618/]Tom Holland[/url] as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Spider-Man]Spider-Man[/url] into the MCU. Oh, and a character many of us weren't that familiar with, [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Panther]Black Panther[/url].

This was my favourite MCU film until "Infinity War/Endgame". A *MUST* watch!

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3480822/]Black Widow[/url] - Released 2021 - 5/5[/size]

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0424060/]Scarlett Johansson[/url] finally gets a standalone movie for her character. I watched it (again) just last week, and it definitely stands up to be counted among the better MCU films.

We meet Black Widow's (adoptive) sister, [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Yelena_Belova]Yelena Belova[/url], played by the supremely-watchable [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm6073955/]Florence Pugh[/url]; and their adoptive father, [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Guardian]Alexei Shostakov[/url], played by [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1092086/]David Harbour[/url]. He is very funny as "Red Guardian", the Soviet Union's first official super-soldier, who is obsessed by Captain America, the foe he was intended to match, but whom he has never encountered.

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2250912/]Spider-Man: Homecoming[/url] - Released 2017 - 4/5[/size]

After Disney reached its agreement with Sony to borrow the rights to Spider-Man (Marvel sold those rights in the 1990's), that character gets a very good movie as his first outing. Most notable for [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000474/]Michael Keaton's[/url] performance as the [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Vulture]Vulture[/url].

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1825683/]Black Panther[/url] - Released 2018 - 5/5[/size]

The late [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1569276/]Chadwick Boseman[/url] gets his character's first full outing in a superbly-crafted and delivered movie. Supported by:

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0430107/]Michael B. Jordan[/url] as [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Erik_Killmonger]Killmonger[/url]

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm2143282/]Lupita Nyong'o[/url] (too beautiful to find words for...)

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1775091/]Danai Gurira[/url] as the tough-as-fuck general leading Wakanda's military forces

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0293509/]Martin Freeman[/url] (Tim from [url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0290978/]The Office (UK)[/url])

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm2257207/]Daniel Kaluuya[/url] (formerly [url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJBzOe_dv24]Parking Pataweyo[/url]  :D )

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm4004793/]Letitia Wright[/url] stealing every scene she is in as Black Panther's little sister

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0785227/]Andy Serkis[/url] reprises his character from "Age of Ultron" to excellent effect!

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm6328300/]Winston Duke[/url] gives a great performance in the character role I would have liked to play, if I had African heritage...

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001845/]Forest Whitaker[/url] plays the holy man, with the only dodgy accent in the whole movie...

And the lady I would amputate my own right arm to get close to, [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000291/]Angela Bassett[/url], plays Black Panther's mum.

Superbly produced and directed, with music by Kendrick Lamar, who my son tells me is quite popular with the kids these days...

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1211837/]Doctor Strange[/url] - Released 2016 - 5/5[/size]

Another must see. (And if you haven't already seen it, I'll want to know why...  :x )

This is all about magic. Well, sorcery to be exact.

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1212722/]Benedict Cumberbatch[/url] is *PERFECTLY* cast - I cannot think of any actor who could have done justice to [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Doctor_Strange]Doctor Strange[/url].

His (sort of) girlfriend is played [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm1046097/]Rachel McAdams[/url], who I am deeply, deeply in Love with...

[url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0842770/]Tilda Swinton[/url] gives an impeccable performance as the [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0842770/]Ancient One[/url], who is the current Sorcerer Supreme when Strange first shows up; and Strange's soon-to-be dependable wingman [url=https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Wong]Wong[/url] is played by [url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nXhZIyGTz5Q]"The IT Crowd"[/url]'s [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0938950/]Benedict Wong[/url].

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3501632/]Thor: Ragnarok[/url] - Released 2017 - 5/5[/size]

I wasn't a fan of this when I first saw it, 'cos I didn't like the way that the Thor character was being developed...

But the fact of the matter is that it is directed by the legendary [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0169806/]Taika Waititi[/url], has the divine [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000949/]Cate Blanchett[/url] in it as the Goddess of Death, and is actually a pretty good team-up between Thor and Hulk (it's what they were doing while the rest of the Avengers were having their Civil War on Earth).

The post-credits "sting" leads directly into "Infinity War", so you kind of have to watch it, just for that.

[size=150][url=https://www.imdb.com/title/tt5095030/]Ant-Man and the Wasp[/url] - Released 2018 - 4/5[/size]

Last (chronologically) MCU movie before "Infinity War". Introduces [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000201/]Michelle Pfeiffer[/url] into the MCU (as Hank Pym's wife); [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0671567/]Michael Peña[/url] is very fucking funny in it; and they even manage to find space for [url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000401/]Laurence Fishburne[/url], yay!

...

Right, that's it then. Lots of other MCU movies after "Endgame", and I haven't touched on the numerous TV series, which you can watch after you have followed the menu above and fully appreciated "Infinity War/Endgame" as the Greatest Movie of All Time!

Enjoy.
