PETITION FOR PEACE & PROSPERITY

As we seek to create Harmony in our World, We, the undersigned, hereby publicly commit to taking the Five Steps to Peace & Prosperity:

We acknowledge that certain old beliefs about Life are no longer working.

We acknowledge that there is something we do not understand about Life, the understanding of which could change Everything.

We are willing for new understandings of Life to now be brought forth, understandings that could produce a new Way of Life on this Planet.

We are willing to explore and examine these new understandings, and, if they align with our Inner Truth and Knowledge, to enlarge our Belief System to include them.

We are willing to live our lives as a Demonstration of our Beliefs.
