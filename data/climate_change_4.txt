I'll be honest, I haven't studied the suggested "solutions" to man-made Climate Change any more than I have the data that details it. So I won't wax lyrical with an opinion on "Cap & Trade" that I don't have.

I'll say only that I'm in broad agreement with you. It sounds like a half-assed, politicised fudge that doesn't sound very workable, for the reasons you state.

I might say that I think that something is better than nothing though. And since the "disease" is Humanity's self-destructive tendencies, any palliative that reduces or off-sets the effects of that disease can't really be said to be worse than the disease itself.

Unless that is you consider that "Cap & Trade" as proposed will increase or accelerate Humanity's demise at his own hands, in which case common sense would force me to agree with you. But like I said, I've not paid any attention to "Cap & Trade", so I bow to your expert professional knowledge on the topic (not that I'm looking for an argument on the subject in any case...).

To my mind, the issue that Humanity faces right now is far deeper and more fundamental than simply managing our carbon dioxide output. I think like you, I feel the obsession with Climate Change narrows the focus away from a much broader challenge.

And that challenge is how Humanity can build a [b]*COMPLETELY*[/b] sustainable existence on this Planet.

As a Society, 21st Century Humanity exists completely out of Harmony with the Environment it relies upon to survive. Man-made Climate Change is a key problem, but so too are over-population, top-soil depletion, water shortages, pollution, bio-diversity in the Ecosphere, depletion of other vital natural resources, acid rain, hazardous waste and all manner of other factors.

Our primary objective as a species, as far as I can see, [b]*MUST*[/b] be to build a fully sustainable existence.

It is a problem that we are really the first humans in our History to have to face. Always before there were new territories to discover, utilise and exploit. But we have now reached the limits of the closed system that is our Planet's eco-sphere. There is nowhere left to expand into, and there is nowhere left to hide the waste that we create.

I'm not talking about saving the Planet for all the other Earth-based species: that is a positive goal, but can only be incidental to saving our own species. But as a natural species ourselves we have to re-establish our connection to the Natural World in order to establish a sustainable harmony with it.

In the West particularly, and certainly since the Industrial Revolution, we have all been born to a Culture that tells us that we exert complete domination over the Natural World (even as we depend on that Natural World's ability to support our Existence).

But I can't help but see that we seem to have lost all sense of humility when it comes to our sense of imperviousness to Natural Change, and our reliance on a stable Ecosphere.

And I've just got a sneaky feeling that if we don't change our ways to reintegrate ourselves with the Natural Order of Things; then the Natural Order of Things may just change its ways, in a manner that we may not find entirely to our liking.

I'll argue with anybody about anything, as you know. But I think that getting into an argument with a Planet might just be one argument too far for me.

Know what I mean?
