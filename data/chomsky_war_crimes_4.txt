The World and its population face many challenges, challenges that require popular solutions. No-one, not even the corporate sponsors of our politicians are in complete possession of the facts, thus we must all necessarily rely on limited information.

When we discuss Humanity's problems with each other, we have the choice to examine the actual issues, based on the information we have available to us, in a calm, rational and logical way.
