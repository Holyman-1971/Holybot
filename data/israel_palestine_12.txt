You know, one of the worst things about wedding ourselves to a particular ideology or allegiance, is the way it forces us to suspend intellectual rigour and close our minds, when we look at complex issues.

Such a position is self-reinforcing, since the suspension of our rational faculty makes complex issues seem utterly intractable; and faced with such intractability, we determine the best course of action to be to just commit to one side or the other of the complex issue and hope that our support will help our chosen side to prevail.

It becomes then, not an intellectual pursuit or even an exercise in morality, to examine or discuss the merits of the complex issue, but an Emotional activity into which we invest our identities and personalities.

All available evidence surrounding the Israel-Palestine issue clearly indicates that the Modern State of Israel holds the upper hand in its Conflict with the Palestinians. But those of us who have invested our personal credibility in supporting Israel must ignore this overwhelming weight of evidence, and twist our intelligence and understanding around torturous, conflicting and contradictory statements that attempt to maintain our sense of self-righteousness.
