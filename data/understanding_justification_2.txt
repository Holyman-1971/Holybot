It's just practice Drew, that's all it is. And it is something that I totally practice (and have practiced) on forums like these.

Clear communication can and does help us win respect and influence with our peers; but far more important than that, is that it clarifies our own thoughts to ourselves.

Practicing clear communication then, is not about getting our points accepted by others, but articulating them to our own satisfaction. And that just takes practice. The more one does it, the better one gets at it.

Think of it like mental jogging. It isn't gift, natural ability or determination that makes one physically or mentally fit, it's just exercise.

We do tend to hit a point around our twenties, where we figure we've learned everything we need to know, and we switch off. And alright, if we've got some strong ideas to begin with, it'll take a while before we're completely weak, intellectually speaking. But for the main part, the lack of exercise for our minds, is as unhealthy and self-destructive as lack of exercise for our bodies.

And it's not a question of how fit we each are, just a case of being fit, or not. So long as we exercise, we're fit, for a given and accepted level of "fit": Body, Mind, and Spirit, if that last is your bag.

And it's just exercise, exercise and practice.

At 't end of 't day, it doesn't really matter in what level of opinion our friends hold us in: that they have an opinion of us at all, counts as a form of friendship. Therefore, when we speak or post publicly, we're not really that interested in acclamation or approval; but we are interested in getting our ideas straight for ourselves.

And that's just something that everyone can do, with practice.
