In fairness to President Obama, if his call for the World "to stand up" was a call for greater multilateralism, then I fully support his aims.

I think that the World is a much smaller place these days, and to me, the World's Nations are analogous to our own local communities.

If we had a single neighbour who was cutting up rough, behaving in a decidedly anti-social manner, then we couldn't and shouldn't rely on just another single neighbour to always take responsibility for restraining the anti-social element. The only way to deliver a credible, authoritative and hopefully lasting settlement would be for us all to act together to explain, demonstrate and ultimately demand that the errant neighbour behave in an acceptable fashion.

It's the same in the World.

There are other countries, like Myanmar, Somalia, Sudan, Saudi Arabia, Belarus and Uzbekistan whose rulers are as despicable as those of North Korea. As a civilised World Community of Human Beings, we should be able to agree on a suitable apparatus for legitimately intervening in the affairs of sovereign nations, with the purpose of ensuring that Human Rights are respected.

This can't be done by a single power acting unilaterally, regardless of whether that power is the U.S., China, Russia or any other nation.

It isn't about military might or capacity; it is about legitimate authority.

We all learned how to do this in our own countries, turning them from lawless feudal states and frontiers into lawfully ordered communities. We just need to figure out now how to do this as a World Community.
