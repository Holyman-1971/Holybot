Dear Wall Street,

Lovely to hear from you, welcome back to the Human Race.

You are still Humans after all, and we are Human Beings. It's our job to live our lives. Oh sure, money is useful to us too, but it's not our religion. We're mostly concerned with having fun, hanging out with our friends and families, enjoying our waking lives, and exploring our breath-taking World.

We're quite relieved that "The Market" we always knew was crap anyway, has finally revealed itself to be undeniably so. As a species, we have many diverse challenges to face together, and we know that most of those challenges are about Collective Survival rather than individual material glory.

Indeed, many of us are only too willing to give up our time and contribute our energy to helping one another out, with no thought of any material gain. We are happy to do it for friendship, for Love, and with the knowledge that one day we might need help ourselves. We'd rather rely on those who will give us that help for the Common Good, rather than because there is an opportunity for personal gain in it for them.

You know, much like when We, the Human Race, gave all our money to stop Wall Street going bankrupt, which we know from past experience would have had you all throwing yourselves to your deaths.

Because money *IS* that important to you.

Anyway, really looking forward to having you bright young things re-join our Community, bringing with you all that experience and wisdom about Real Life that will see you easily walk into and hold down a Real Job at last.

And of course, since you won't be hogging all of Humanity's Wealth amongst a tiny clique of self-protecting insiders, we're also looking forward to seeing a bit more of that spread around too.

Love and Peace,

Humanity
