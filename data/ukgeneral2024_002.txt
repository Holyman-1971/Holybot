Resolve the NHS crisis

Resolve the Education crisis

Resolve the Cost-of-Living crisis that continues to economically shatter the lives of so many UK citizens.

Everything else is of secondary importance to the overwhelming majority of UK voters. The focus on immigration during the campaign was primarily because of the Reform Party's involvement, but also because the Conservative Party could not discuss any other topic without shame and embarrassment.

And the Labour Party could not discuss *ANYTHING*. This election was always the Conservative Party's to lose, all that the Labour Party needed to do was ensure its spokespeople said very little about actual policy. That strategy, coupled with very, very effective targeting of key seats, has given the incoming Labour Government a very, very useful Parliamentary majority for the next 5 years.

We will see how they get on with things.
