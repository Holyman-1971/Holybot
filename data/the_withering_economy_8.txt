As best as I can tell, both the Republicans and the Democrats, Presidentially stretching back through Clinton, Bush I, Reagan and Carter, and Congressionally over much the same period, have all participated in allowing and in many cases encouraging this crisis to take place.

No, what I'm talking about is the fact that President Bush (and the Republican and Democrat Congress) sat by whilst this situation assumed epic proportions, and failed to act, either through incompetence, negligence, or criminal intent.

Let me try an analogy, and see if I can get through that thick skull of yours.

Imagine that you have been appointed Fire Warden of a junior grade school. The post has an initial four year tenure, renewable for a further four years after that, depending on your performance and the satisfaction of the school authorities.

Your job as Fire Warden is to try and ensure that no fires break out in the school. If a fire does break out, then it is your responsibility to ensure the safety of all the children and staff in the school. And whilst you will do everything you can to make sure that a fire does not break out, since you can't guarantee it won't - there are always arsonists to consider - you must make sure that all the necessary safety equipment and escape facilities are in place and fit for purpose.

OK, now imagine that you have been in your job as a Fire Warden for coming on eight years, nearly at the end of your term.

Looking back over your tenure, you are aware that when you first took on the job, the risk of fire was very great. You are also aware that fire safety precautions were less than adequate, and that provisions for the safety of the children and staff in the school were very poor.

Whatever else you did during your time as Fire Warden, and whatever else you planned to achieve during your time as Fire Warden, it would all be for nothing, if a fire broke out in the school, destroying the school buildings, and causing great loss and suffering to the children and staff.

And remember, tasked with such a great responsibility, you had almost completely unrestricted power to implement and enforce whatever safety measures you felt necessary. As essentially the Most Powerful Man in the School, you could pretty much do whatever it is you wanted to do, no matter how much it cost, to preserve and protect the safety of The School, its children and its staff.

Recognising the clearly inadequate state of Fire Safety at the School when you took over the job, and recognising the inadequacy of the fire precautions and safety facilities, you personally were the Man most responsible for improving the situation, by whatever means necessary.

Why, if you wanted to, you were even permitted to go outside of the School, and hunt down anyone you even suspected might be considering setting fire to the School! You had [b]*THAT*[/b] much power, [b]*THAT*[/b] much authority, and a [b]*HUGE*[/b] amount of economic and human resources at your disposal to get the job done.

More, in fact, than anyone else, anywhere.

And now, nearly at the end of your time as the School's Fire Warden, a Fire [b]*HAS*[/b] broken out.

And that Fire [b]*IS*[/b] destroying the School Buildings.

And that Fire [b]*IS*[/b] causing loss and suffering amongst the Children and Staff of the School.

Now...
