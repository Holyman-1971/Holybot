The Internet is, as it always has been and always will be, a conceptualised infrastructure that inter-connects *ALL*.

Critically, no single human being or collaborating group of human beings can control, govern or own the Internet; or what takes place on the ever-evolving plasma of the physical and digital infrastructure that operates the Internet Concept.

As individual human beings adjust to this new socio-economic Reality, there will be attempts by other individuals (and other networks of individuals) to try and capitalise on the service-delivery possibilities provided by the Internet. We have seen this taking place very visibly, during the first quarter of this current century.

This is possible (and largely unavoidable) because of the disproportionate power wielded by certain individuals and networks of individuals. And because they currently possess what they believe is sufficient "power" to prevent any loss of it, they will use that "power" in the only way they know how to use it: for direction, coercion and domination.

These individuals saw the Internet and immediately (and compulsively) identified its possibilities for making those individuals enormous amounts of money. Consequently, the first two decades of the 21st century have been about [url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_ice_cream_wars ]"Ice-Cream Wars"[/url] between the likes of Facebook, Apple, Amazon and Google/Meta.

(Without the specific organisation names/"brands", exactly as the authors of "Netocracy" predicted in 2001 would happen.)

And we "Netizens" lapped it all up, for the most part.

Even those Individuals who weren't connected to (and therefore potentially contaminated by) the Internet, couldn't help but Experience its impact on Humanity's 21st Century. Not least any Individual who had been remorselessly consumerised for all of their lives, especially those Individuals living in Humanity's "West".

The deliberate, policy-driven, industrial-scale consumerisation of Western Society following 1945, conditioned many citizens of the "West" to derive their personal sense of fulfilment from the relentless acquisition of "new" material goods and monetary "wealth".

By the year 2000, this was catastrophically exhausting the Planet's ability to keep up. So the deliberately generated and maintained appetite for mass consumption was transposed from material goods to services and "content" delivered via the Internet.

(I know that for a fact, because I have helped several organisations achieve it; so have you Slacks.)

And here we are now, in the Human World year of 2024; and regarding the Current State of Humanity three decades after the emergence of the World Wide Web (All Hail TBL!)...

I mean...

WTF? :shock:

But Never Fear.

The Individuals concerned have now exhausted any delusions they may once have had, about exploiting the functionality provided by the Internet for their personal gain at others' expense.

The Sewage Line on the Internet is dropping.

And as the amount of sewage decreases (i.e. is ignored), Individuals will be able to communicate more effectively with one another in their own Networks, because they will waste less and less Time having to fish through sewage.
