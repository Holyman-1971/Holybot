Let's be clear here, torture "worked for 1,000's of years", because the purpose of torture was never to extract useable information.

Most commonly, it was used to force someone to sign a confession (to crimes they had or had not committed); or, in the case of the Spanish Inquisition, for example, to force someone to publicly change their beliefs or religion.

But torture was also used as a deterrent. For the average medieval peasant, locking them up in jail was more of an improvement in their living conditions: at the least, they were assured of food and shelter, just to prolong their imprisonment.

Capital punishment was liberally applied, to all manner of offences, but again, given the poor health and low life expectancy, a death sentence was often a blessed relief. And besides, if the alternative to not stealing a sheep was a slow death from starvation, risking a speedy death from hanging, was probably well worth it.

No, the purpose of torture has always been the same: to indicate clearly that whilst a captive may eventually die - and most ideologues welcome martyrdom - it will be a long and painful process, creating more suffering than the torture victim has ever known.

What use, when fighting Islamic Jihadis willing to martyr their selves in suicide missions, is threatening Capital Punishment?

No, the reason why the Bush Administration is using torture, and wants to retroactively legalise its past illegal use of torture, is because the only possible disincentive to fight, for the people the US is fighting, is the prospect of a horrible, painful, drawn-out, suffering death.

All modern intelligence agencies concur that information extracted under torture, is utterly unreliable, and most frequently, useless.

Torture isn't used by the Bush Administration to extract information that would otherwise be unavailable: it is used in an attempt to frighten and cower the people it is fighting.

Unfortunately, this is just another hypocritical nail in the coffin of the Bush Administration's professed aims in the Middle East.
