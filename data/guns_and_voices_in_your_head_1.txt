[quote="Holyman"][color=#0000FF]And if, on the other hand, you select as your position a belief in the immutable and unknowable mysteries surrounding the desires and intentions of a Supreme Being... Faith is distinct from knowledge in that the definition of "faith" is the belief in something for which you have no proof. Indeed, evidence is the enemy of Faith, because you cannot have faith in something for which you have proof.

So you either have evidence, or you have Faith: but you can't have both.

And Faith is all well and good when it comes to a belief in the Existence of an Omnipotent, Omniscient and Omnipresent Deity, because you are basically saying that you believe in something that is beyond human comprehension. The very fact that God is so boundlessly magnificent and possesses such awe inspiring power, means that human beings aren't fit to even gaze upon His countenance, much less interpret his specific wishes and desires.

Now if you have Faith in the God of Abraham, then you have Faith in the Pentateuch. And that means you believe in the specific instructions of the Ten Commandments, as the Word of God. That's fine, that's what you signed up to: you can interpret the Ten Commandments and apply them how you will; steering clear of the obvious false idolatry, bovine covetousness and for the most part, the killing.

But what you cannot do is point to any equivalent doctrinal instructions that explicitly reveals how God feels about firearms legislation in 21st Century America. The Abrahamic Canon is a bit light on specifics about that.

So, either you make your own mind up about how God feels about that particular subject, and then retroactively and somewhat arrogantly apply God's approval to your own thoughts on the matter (which is more like saying that God has Faith in you, rather than your having Faith in Him)...

Or, you listen very carefully to the Voice in your head that is telling you What God Wants You to Do.

And that's really the point I was making:

If you are hearing voices in your head telling you what to do, you shouldn't be allowed within a hundred miles of anything more dangerous than a bath sponge.
