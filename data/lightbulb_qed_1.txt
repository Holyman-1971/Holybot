The thing is, the Lightbulb has got to really want to change itself.

It's all very well Everyone else offering to help, but it doesn't matter if it is One or Many: if the Lightbulb itself doesn't want to change, no amount of effort on *ANYONE'S* part is going to be able to change it.

It doesn't matter whether you try to encourage it to change, by showing it all manner of luxuries and advances; or even try coercing it to change, threatening it or trying to force it to change.

If the Lightbulb doesn't want to change, you can change the Entire Universe around the Lightbulb, but the Lightbulb will STILL insist that there is no reason to change.

It is hard to tell a Lightbulb that has spent its entire Existence in darkness, what Being Switched On feels like. It is hard to sell it on the Benefits of Being Switched On, especially if it has been in the Dark for so long that the Benefits of Being Switched On blinds it with glare, and makes it plant itself so firmly in its slot, that neither Edison nor bayonet could shift it.

No, you've got to give the Lightbulb a Reason to change.

And you can only give it a Reason to change that it will understand; if you first understand its reasons.

If you can understand why the Lightbulb doesn't want to change in the first place, helping the Lightbulb understand good reasons for change becomes so much easier.

Frequently, if you give a Lightbulb a good enough Reason: It will change Itself.

Threatening a Broken Lightbulb with Eternal Darkness, is no Reason for a Lightbulb to want to change Itself.

And it is not a threat that frightens a Broken Lightbulb.

Understand why the Lightbulb thinks it is broken; and help the Lightbulb understand that it is not, in fact, broken: it just needs to Change, and then it will work just fine.

And when the Lightbulb realises this:

The Lightbulb will Change Itself. :)
