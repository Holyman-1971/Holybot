Hey Dime,

So, I managed to carve out a bit of Time. It's no chore, because I have had you and your family in my prayers this past week, and you have been in my thoughts throughout.

Like you, I have given close attention to the messages that God has been sending [b]*ALL*[/b] of us, throughout our History together. And like you, I recognise that these messages have been filtered through cultural, social, political, and above all, Human filters since Time began.

The variations we find in each culture are, as I know you understand, on precisely the same Theme; only appearing to be different, because of how unique our individual and collective consciousnesses are.

I [b]*DO*[/b] realise that the Truth is One Thing, and Everything; that the Truth is defined and recognised by our own unique observance.

And I understand that the Truth is... Love.

Unconditional Love.

The Love of a Parent for a Child; of a Child for a Parent.

What we make of that Truth, what we [b]*HAVE*[/b] made of that Truth, has been, and will continue to be whatever we imagine we can make of that Truth.

Love is an Energy: it is [b]*THE*[/b] Energy from which we are all composed. God [b]*IS*[/b] that Love, that Energy. It is from that Energy, that Love, that we were brought into Being, in that Perfect, Creative Moment of Love; and it is to Love that we [b]*WILL*[/b] all return.

Together, and in Perfect Union and Harmony.

And it is that Return to Union that I'd like to talk about now, with you.

When I was 17 years old, I was... A very angry young man.

Not externally or visibly angry - my friends and family felt I was a relaxed and gentle person. But by myself, in those moments of introspection that can dominate a teenager's life, I [b]*WAS*[/b] a very angry person.

I was angry because when I was just eight years old, by older (by eighteen months) brother, Christopher, died in a tragic accident. The kind of accident that a 10 year old boy has, because he ignores his parents' warnings about what kind of things are dangerous to do. In Christopher's case, he ignored the warning not to tie things around his neck. He was playing by himself at my grandparents' house, whilst my grandmother was at work, and my (retired) grandfather was dozing in front of the television.

He was playing a game that involved tying a length of fabric and a dressing gown cord together, to simulate the rope that naval crewman lower themselves from helicopters from to rescue people from the sea. He tripped on the stairs where he was playing his game, and the "rope" he had made slipped up around his neck, and he accidentally hanged himself.

For a long time then, as I grew up, there was a huge void in my life, emptied of my big brother's - my idol's - energy. And I could find nothing to fill that void with, but a deep sense of anger at the unfairness of it all.

I didn't care for much, not even my two younger brothers. I couldn't figure out the point of doing so, since it seemed that Life was so precarious, and Death so impartial, that at any moment, anyone I cared about could be taken from my Life without notice or warning.

I became a very reckless, nihilistic young man, and developed a very unhealthy streak of self-destructiveness within me.

It wasn't long then, before that self-destructiveness brought me to a relationship with drugs, and solvents, and other methods of turning my back on this unfair World, by getting as "High" (...Low) as I could.

And finally, a few months before my 18th birthday, I managed to destroy my Self, and I died from asphyxiation brought on by my inhaling aerosol propellant.

I didn't notice, or realise that I had died.

Getting "High" was something I did in private, so there was no-one else around to respond to my death, to give me any clues that I had in fact died.

And I didn't notice or realise that I had died...

Because we don't.

Not consciously, anyway.

Our bodies die, and our Spirit leaves that vehicle we have grown so attached to. And our Ego fades away too, since we have no further use for that cognitive apparatus, when we are released to our Pure, Eternal Spirit form.

What I perceived in my still conscious state also changed.

The "Reality" in which we all abide, melted away, and as it did, I understood how insubstantial it all really is; how it is as much of an Illusion as a movie or video game. I understood, almost immediately, that it was indeed a parallax: a cognitive apparition, created for our own benefit, by ourselves; as insubstantial and illusory as any dream or hallucination.

And I realised that I was returning to the True Reality; the True Reality of Pure Energy, and Pure Love.

As everything melted away, I saw/felt that every aspect of Energy in Existence possessed a unique but at the same time collective and harmonised identity. Rather than seeing shapes and recognising faces, I felt the Emotional Identity that Everything that Exists possesses.

There are those alive amongst us today who can see a person's aura, around, above and within a person's body; what I saw - or rather felt - was solely the aura, with no body or ego to filter or obstruct the purity of that Soul Energy. I felt the distinct personalities of these energies, and I recognised quite a few of them too, including my brother.

And I felt only Love.

No Fear whatsoever.

Indeed, I realised and fully understood that there really is no such thing as Fear: only the absence of Love.

And I heard/felt many voices saying, "He's not ready."

The next thing I became aware of - and it is difficult to construct a narrative about my Experience, because "Time" is as much an Illusion created by ourselves as "Space" - was that I was reviewing the entirety of my Life up to that point.

It is said that when you die, your Life flashes before your eyes, and I can testify that there is quite a bit of Truth to that idea. But rather than the entirety of my Life merely being replayed before my eyes in an instant, what I felt - rather than saw - was the Emotional record of everything I had done, everything I had been, every Experience I'd had.

What I wasn't prepared for though, was that I also re-experienced every moment in my Life, not just from my own Emotional perspective, but from the perspectives of everyone else whose Life I had affected - positively and negatively. I felt what I had caused - consciously or unconsciously - everyone else I had encountered to feel.

And [b]*THAT*[/b] was an eye-opener, I can tell you.

At the end of this review, I found myself really beginning to understand what had happened to me. I fully understood that my Experience of "Life" had ended, and I had returned to the Eternal State from which we all come, and to which we all return.

And upon this realisation, the first thing that I thought of, was my mother.

I thought about how she had already buried one son in her Life.

And I knew in that instant, in that Moment, that I absolutely did not want her to have to bury another.

My Love for her demanded that I spare her that.

And in that instant of realisation, the Experience I had been having since "dying" ended; and in a flash, I returned to my body, to this Waking Dream we call Life.

I sat bolt upright in my body, crying as I hadn't cried since my brother died: crying with Love.

I checked my heart to see that I really was alive, and found it beating at an incredible rate. My lungs were drawing in great gulps of air, and every breath brought a renewed energy and a renewed vitality back into my body.

I was Alive again.

Nobody had witnessed my "death". No friends, family or medical professional. Afterwards, I only told a couple of very close friends what had happened, because at the time, I don't think I fully appreciated just exactly what had happened. Whilst it was totally unlike any dream or hallucination I had ever had, there was no-one in my Life who'd had a comparable Experience, with whom I could compare my Experience.

But the Experience did have a profound effect on how I felt about Life: about my Life, and the Life of Everyone around me.

I dropped out of college immediately, and actually joined the Army. Though I think I understood at the time I was a Pacifist; my capacity for being judgemental about my own choices, or the choice of others had disappeared. And I felt that being in the Military was [b]*PRECISELY*[/b] where a Pacifist should be.

As it turned out, however, a perforated ear-drum prevented me from joining the Army Air Corps, my preferred choice to fly support helicopters. And by the time the medical process had run its course, I'd discovered both the Love of a girlfriend, and a Love of computer systems with which I felt I could make a much better contribution to changing the World for the better.

I understood that there was nothing to Fear from God. I understood that the only judgement that awaits us, post mortem, is any judgement we might feel compelled to pass on ourselves.

I understood that Everything in our World is an Illusion; but an Illusion with a Purpose. And that Purpose is to help us Experience and Understand... [b]*WHO WE REALLY ARE*[/b].

And I understood that the Greatest Illusion, is the Illusion of Separation.

The Illusion that we are in anyway separated from each other, or from God.

I understood that we [b]*ARE*[/b] God, Experiencing God's Self, by subjectively individuating as infinitely different aspects of God.

There is Nothing that Exists that is [b]*NOT*[/b] God.

We [b]*ARE*[/b] all One Consciousness, Experiencing Itself Subjectively, through the Dream of "Reality".

And we are all Love.

The Energy that is God [b]*IS*[/b] Love.

The most potent and [b]*ONLY*[/b] Force and Power that Exists: Unconditional Love.

In 2001, I became much more curious about exploring the nature of the Experience I'd had when I was 17, and I joined an Email forum that discussed and explored the Near Death Experience - or NDE.

About a thousand members strong (and still growing), about a hundred of those members had all had an NDE. Through reading and participating in discussions with those members, I found so much commonality between my Experience and theirs, that I finally put to rest any doubts I'd had that I had in fact crossed through the Curtain of Illusion that - apparently - separates us from the Divine Unity we know as Heaven.

Heaven is not a separate place, at one remove from our Reality. Heaven is all around us. The Souls of Everyone we ever knew, and ever will know, surround us always, never departing. It is only we - the "Living" - who can imagine that we are, now, in anyway separate or removed from our Eternal Existence as aspects of God.

We choose our "Lives" to teach us those things about ourselves that we cannot fully understand, when we can only know ourselves as Truly Eternal Beings.

To understand who and what we are, we must first be able to Experience ourselves as who and what we are [b]*NOT*[/b]; and then re-member ourselves as who we Truly Are. It is in the Return to our Divine and Eternal State that we can fully appreciate and understand what it means to be a part of God.

And we can only Experience that "Return" to Being a part of God; if we can imagine ourselves being [b]*APART*[/b] from God.

Everything I have Experienced since my NDE, since fully appreciating the nature of my NDE, has only confirmed and reinforced these views, at every step of the way. It is not a question of Faith, or dogmatic belief: but a concrete understanding that it is the only Meaning of Life that makes any sense.

I'm not going to try and relate anything I have Experienced, or anything I understand, to your Experience Dime, or the specific Experience you are having right now. Our Experiences are connected only by Love, not by any shared detail; details which you and I each make up for our own benefit.
