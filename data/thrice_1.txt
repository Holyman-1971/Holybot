The point I was making about your rage and anger was really addressing the way that you described it coming out. From what you wrote, it really does sound as though you "lost it". And it is in "losing it" that our anger and rage often creates bigger problems for us.

We all experience anger, and in fact, it is a vital part of our psychological and spiritual function. But if we want to avoid our anger creating even more reasons for us to feel angry, we've got to control how our anger expresses itself. And that usually means not directing our anger at specific individuals, or even society in general. Simply put, it doesn't matter how much we're hurting ourselves, hurting others isn't going to make us hurt less, usually it's quite the opposite.

On the subject of paedophilia, the fact is that Society just hasn't figured out a solution to it yet. By that I mean there is no agreed strategy for combatting paedophilia, so I think it *is* beholden on individual members of Society to make short-term, tactical decisions on how to deal with it.

I believe we can and certainly should try to understand why a paedophile does the things he does. Not so that we can excuse or justify their actions, but so that we can try to develop that better understanding of how to deal with and prevent such abuse in the future.

As far as I'm concerned, there is a social compact that we all enter into, as long as we choose to involve ourselves in Society and partake of its benefits. A huge part of that compact is that as an adult, you simply just don't even consider children in a sexual perspective. If you do, and if you act upon that consideration, then you have broken the compact, the trust that Society once extended you. And, you can never replace that trust once it has been destroyed. If you get caught just once abusing children, you can and must never again be trusted to be around children.

That then is the practical purpose of punishing paedophiles, of locking them away: they have shown their selves to be untrustworthy around children; and they must be prevented from ever harming another child for the rest of their lives.

Now do we kill them? Beat them? Castrate them? Physically punish them in anyway?

I'm not really sure what it is we get as a Society from doing that. I know as an individual, it gives us a sense of moral righteousness to kick the shit out of child-abusing scum. But short of making us feel a little better, for a short amount of time, it doesn't really achieve anything.

Further physical abuse from those sickened by their actions, can have the effect of further legitimising and excusing their actions.
