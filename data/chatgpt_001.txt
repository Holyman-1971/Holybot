Write a story about a group of space pirates, patrolling the galaxy in their ship called "The Original Post".

Captain Slacks is the pirate chief and captain of "The Original Post".

Commander Foota is Quartermaster.

Lieutenant-Commander Xerxes is the ship's Navigator.

Lieutenant-Commander Htown is the ship's Chief of the Watch.

Lieutenant-Commander Bunny is the ship's Carpenter.

Mr. Holyman is a civilian travelling as the ship's Observer.

The story should describe an adventure involving the whole crew attacking a trade galleon. The galleon is called "The Donald". The galleon is under the command of Captain Elon Musk. The galleon's cargo is gold and human slaves.

The story should include separate actions for each crew member. They story should also include two actions where three or more crew members work together successfully.

The crew of "The Original Post" successfully attack "The Donald" and capture many chests full of gold. They also capture Captain Elon Musk and will hold him for a large ransom payment. They release all the slaves and give them control of "The Donald", after renaming it "Old Joe".

The story ends with a victory feast onboard "The Original Post".
