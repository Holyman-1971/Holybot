You know Foota, when I'm sharing conversations with People about Spiritual Growth, I often talk about what I call the "Cookie Jar Moment".

The "Cookie Jar Moment" is a way to remind People that Growth - of whatever form - is a continual, constant process of Change.

There are moments, with Growth, where suddenly you can understand something, or do something, or believe in something that you couldn't do before you reached a certain stage of Growth.

Imagine you're a child, growing taller each day. There's a cookie jar, on a shelf in the kitchen, which in the early stages of your childhood growth, you just can't reach. But one day, you wake up, and you find you've grown tall enough to reach the Cookie Jar, and all of a sudden, your Life takes on a whole new, Cookie-filled dimension.

And you think "Wow! I'm now capable of eating as many cookies as I want!"

And you think that's an amazing thing: your Life is now full of Cookies.

But you forget that Life isn't about Cookies, it's about Growth. And just because you're now tall enough to reach the Cookie Jar, it doesn't mean you've finished growing: it just means you've reached, and are now passing, the Cookie Jar Moment. But stay focused on the Growth, rather than the Cookies, and you'll be surprised what else you will discover you can reach.

If the Cookie Jar is a basic and Universal understanding and respect for Human Rights, and the Understanding of the Rights of [b]*ALL*[/b] Humans to one day, have the Opportunity to eat Cookies, then it is clear that Culturally, Sociologically, and Anthropologically... Islamic Culture in the Middle East and Africa, has yet to reach the Cookie Jar Moment.

And neither has the Culture of the United States of America.
