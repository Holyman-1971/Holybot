Such is the way of things, I figured it'd be a nice idea to look back over the last 12 months of PBF activity. This inevitably gave rise to the idea of an End of Year Awards Ceremony, which seems to be pretty much all that ever goes on in January.

So, whilst thinking about other possible awards - and feel free to suggest any - here is one for "PBF R'n'R Thread of the Year".

To make qualification fair and egalitarian (as opposed to just nominating the ones I created or figure prominently in :)), I decided to use the following metrics for nominations for Thread of the Year.

1 - The first and last posts in the thread must both have been in 2006

2 - The thread must have a minimum of 300 replies.

Uh, that's it.

What follows, is a shortlist of potential contenders for "PBF R'n'R Thread of the Year 2006". This poll is intended to select the top three, and assuming a clear winner doesn't emerge (and I've a feeling it may... :)), the top three will go forward to a final vote that will run from 20th January 2007 for a week.
