[b]CURRENT STATE[/b]

Human Society is fractured by beliefs that many people share.

Most of these beliefs are based on ideas first codified and transcribed thousands of years ago.

Transcriptions that have subsequently been through thousands of years of (human) translations and interpretations.

Even (and perhaps especially) with those who share the same root beliefs, there is sharp division between interpretations of those same beliefs.

Individual human beings take advantage of this Disunity to enhance their own power and privilege.

The most prominent and well-known belief systems all seem to share a common theme of Unity. Most (if not all) of the Disunity in the Human World is the direct result of various societies' beliefs about Unity.

This Disunity [i]always[/i] leads to violence.

The violence is [i]always[/i] because of what others believe.

If only others believed what we believe, there would be no violence.

NB. It has ever been thus.

[b]IDEAL STATE[/b]

Human Society is Unfied by the similarities in Everyone's beliefs.

The beliefs are rooted in a rational understanding of contemporary situations, nurtured by scientific and spiritual exploration.

The Freedom to Believe what you want about your own Life is sacred.

You are Free to express what you Believe to anyone who will listen.

Violence is forbidden.

NB. As with most Programmes, the "Likely Future State" will be somewhere short of the "Ideal State", but hopefully closer to that than to the "Current State".
